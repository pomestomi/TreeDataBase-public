TREE PHOTOGRAPHS AND APPRAISAL DOCUMENTATION NOT INCLUDED
=========================================================

Project: City of Potsdam

The photographs of the monitored Potsdam trees — three tree-appraisal
campaigns (2024, June 2025, November 2025) and the soil-profile pictures of
the Puerckhauer soil cores — together with the accompanying appraisal and
soil documentation are not part of the public repository.

This material is made available upon special request to the authors or
curators of this dataset.

Contact: Thomas Maier, thomas.maier@fau.de

Pictures of the monitored trees in multiple seasons, for Potsdam and for the
other deployments, are available on request as well.

Included for these trees are: the sensor tree locations and the hand-digitised
land-use layers (VectorLayers/), the tree attributes, and all events derived
from the sensor measurements. The raw sensor time series of the Potsdam and
Hagen trees are restricted in the same way — see
TreeTabularData/trees/README_RESTRICTED_RAW_DATA.txt.
